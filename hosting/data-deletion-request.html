Account & Data Deletion Request

HotBite Delivery Limited

HotBite allows users to request deletion of their account and personal data. To submit a request, email us with your full name, the email address linked to your HotBite account, and your phone number.

Email a deletion request

Send to support@hotbite.app with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”.

What gets deleted

On verification, we remove or anonymize the personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Profile information (name, email, phone, profile photo)
  • Saved addresses and location data
  • Order and ride history
  • Uploaded images and preferences

What may be retained

Some information may be retained where required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, payment, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

Payment data

HotBite does not store full credit card numbers. Payment processors such as Stripe handle that information securely on our behalf.

Questions about this process? Contact support@hotbite.app.
