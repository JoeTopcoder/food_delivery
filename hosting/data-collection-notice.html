Data Collection Notice

HotBite Delivery Limited

HotBite collects certain information to provide food delivery, ride sharing, grocery delivery, car services, and related features.

Information we collect

  • Names and contact information
  • Delivery addresses and location data
  • Order and ride history
  • Payment records
  • Device information

How we use it

Collected information supports account management, order processing, driver matching, real-time tracking, payment handling, notifications, safety improvements, fraud prevention, support services, and regulatory compliance.

Payment data

HotBite does not store full credit card numbers or CVV codes. Payment processing is delegated to trusted third-party providers such as Stripe.

Deleting your data

You can request removal of your data at hotbite.app/delete-account.
